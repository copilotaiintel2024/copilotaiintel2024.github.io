Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

VPS-Backup is a personal, self-hosted backup utility, not a public service. This notice describes its intended use of Google Drive and related information.

1. Purpose and scope

VPS-Backup is designed to back up server projects, application files, selected databases, and configuration data to a Google Drive account chosen by its owner. This public website provides information only; it does not offer accounts, payments, or a browser-based Google sign-in flow.

2. Google account access

With the owner's explicit authorization, the Rclone client may be granted Google Drive access. The current authorization may include full Drive file access; however, the application's intended use is restricted to uploading, listing, checking, downloading for recovery, and deleting backup files in designated backup locations. It does not intentionally inspect unrelated personal Drive files.

3. Information processed

The backup process may handle files selected for backup (which may include application data), file names and metadata, logs related to backup operations, and OAuth credentials needed to maintain access to Google Drive. Data is processed on the owner's VPS and in the owner's Google Drive account. Encryption before upload is intended to be enabled as part of backup configuration; do not assume data is encrypted until that setup is completed and verified.

4. Data sharing and use

VPS-Backup does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Backup data is transferred to Google Drive at the owner's direction. Google and the VPS hosting provider process data according to their respective services and policies. No other sharing is intended except when required by law or explicitly directed by the account owner.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until removed under the configured retention rules or by the owner. Authorization credentials are stored in the private Rclone configuration on the VPS, not in this public GitHub repository. The owner is responsible for securing the VPS, configuring encryption, restricting access, and testing restore procedures.

6. Access revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke authorization through Google Account connections, remove stored backups from Google Drive, or delete local configuration files. Revoking authorization stops further authorized Google Drive operations until access is granted again.

7. Website hosting

These informational pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information when visitors access this site under GitHub's own privacy terms. This site does not use advertising trackers or contact forms.

8. Updates and contact
